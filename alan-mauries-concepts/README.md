# Alan Mauries · Website concepts

Two mockups with a switcher to present them side by side.

- `index.html` is the switcher. `#hero` and `#gallery` at the end of the URL open a specific concept.
- `hero.html` is the serape + charcoal portrait hero section on its own.
- `gallery.html` is the gallery walk on its own.
- `assets/` holds the images and textures both pages use.

## Putting it on GitHub Pages

1. Copy everything in this folder into a repository, keeping the `assets` folder next to the HTML files.
2. In the repository, open Settings › Pages, choose "Deploy from a branch", pick your branch and the folder these files are in, and save.
3. The site appears at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/` after a minute or two.

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and need nothing else. They also work when opened straight from your computer.

## Notes

- The portrait is a placeholder drawn from a stock photo, and the gallery paintings are stand-ins. Swap in Alan's own work before sharing widely.
- The intro animation in the hero runs about 8.5 seconds. Use Replay in its top-right corner to run it again.
